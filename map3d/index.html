Population Density

Interactive 3D visualization of population distribution across the Indonesian archipelago.

Legend
Spikes: Population density.
Taller height & warmer color (pink) = Higher Density.
Base Map: Air Quality (AQI).
Dark Blue = Good, Dark Red = Poor.
Visual Settings
🖱️ Left Click + Drag to Rotate 🖱️ Right Click + Drag to Pan 🖱️ Scroll to Zoom
